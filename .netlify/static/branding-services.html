Branding FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About Branding

How long does a full brand identity project take?

A complete branding engagement typically takes 3 to 5 weeks. This covers thorough discovery, strategic positioning, initial visual concepts, iterative refinement, and final brand guidelines delivery.

Do we own full intellectual property (IP) rights to our logo and branding?

Yes, 100%. Upon project completion and final payment, full commercial copyright and editable master vector source files (AI, EPS, SVG, PDF, PNG) are handed over to your business.

Can you rebrand an existing company without losing our current recognition?

Absolutely. Rebranding is one of our core specialties. We preserve your existing brand equity while modernizing visual aesthetics, typography, and digital relevance for modern competitive markets.
