Video Production FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About Video Production

Can we send raw phone footage for you to edit into professional reels?

Yes, absolutely! Many of our clients in Boisar, Palghar and Mumbai shoot raw clips on their smartphones and send them via Google Drive. Our editors handle color grading, noise reduction, hook pacing, kinetic captions, and sound design to make it look broadcast-quality.

What is your typical turnaround time for video editing?

Standard reels and short-form video edits are delivered within 24 to 48 hours. Larger commercial brand films or complex 3D motion graphic projects typically take 5 to 7 business days.

What video editing software does your team use?

We use industry-standard suites including Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve Studio (for cinematic color grading), and Blender/Cinema 4D for 3D motion graphics.
