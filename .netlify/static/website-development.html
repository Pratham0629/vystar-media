Frequently Asked Questions About Website Development

How long does it take to design and launch a new business website?

A focused landing page takes 3 to 5 business days. A comprehensive multi-page corporate website or e-commerce store typically takes 2 to 3 weeks from kickoff to live deployment.

Will our website work fast on mobile phones with 4G/5G connections?

Yes! We build modern websites using Next.js, React, and Tailwind CSS with pre-rendered static delivery. Every image is optimized for low mobile data, ensuring sub-second load times on mobile devices.

Do you provide website maintenance and hosting support?

Yes. We offer turnkey hosting on global high-speed CDNs with free SSL certificates, automated daily backups, security monitoring, and ongoing content updates.
