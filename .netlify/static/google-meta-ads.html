Frequently Asked Questions About Paid Ads

What is the minimum recommended ad budget for Google & Meta Ads?

We typically recommend a minimum advertising budget of ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 per month for local campaigns in Boisar and Palghar, and ₹50,000+ for Mumbai/Maharashtra-wide expansion. This provides enough data to test, optimize, and scale profitable conversions.

Which platform is better for my business: Google Ads or Meta Ads?

Google Ads captures active intent (users searching for "best clinic near me" or "industrial supplier in Boisar"). Meta Ads creates demand and builds brand awareness through visual storytelling and video reels. Combining both delivers the highest overall ROI.

How do you ensure leads are qualified and not spam?

We implement custom qualification questions on lead forms, phone number verification, and reCAPTCHA on landing pages. We also track offline CRM conversions back to ad campaigns to optimize for paying customers rather than low-quality clicks.
